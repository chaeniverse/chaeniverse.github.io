I am a Data Scientist on the Big Data Team at [PIPET](https://www.catholic.ac.kr/), The Catholic University of Korea. I received my M.S. and B.S. in Statistics from [Inha University](https://www.inha.ac.kr/), where my graduate research focused on machine learning and neuroscience.

**I work on turning messy real-world data into decisions — and on measuring how well generative AI actually works.** On the healthcare side, I build cohorts from nationwide claims data covering about 50 million people and apply causal inference and survival analysis to clinical questions, for example showing that prior detection of precursor conditions improves survival in multiple myeloma. On the generative AI side, I design and evaluate LLM and image-generation pipelines for a K-pop virtual idol app: chatbot persona design, prompt engineering, LLM model comparison, and image generation.

Before PIPET, I was a data analyst at the Medical Statistics Support Center of Inha University Hospital, supporting clinical researchers with study design, statistical modeling, and manuscript analyses.
